# Google Play ASO — Tower Survival

Updated: 2026-08-01

## Recommended positioning

Pixel idle tower defense with roguelite progression: protect one central tower, survive increasingly dangerous waves, choose run upgrades, and invest earned ore into permanent progression.

The automatic targeting and combat support the `idle defense` positioning. Do not promise passive offline earnings, because that progression system is not implemented yet.

## Free growth plan

1. Verify that the production release is active in target countries and that the device catalog is not excluding most phones.
2. Publish the English and Russian listings together with a meaningful game update.
3. Replace raw gameplay screenshots with six composed screenshots using the captions above.
4. Run one Store Listing Experiment at a time: first icon, then screenshot 1, then short description. Measure retained first-time installers, not only listing conversion.
5. Add an in-app review prompt after a positive moment such as a new record or tier unlock. Never show it on first launch or after defeat.
6. Publish Google Play promotional content for every meaningful update or time-limited challenge.
7. Publish the completed first localization wave (`pt-BR`, `es-419`, `de`, `fr`, `tr`, `id`, `pl`, `it`) with matching localized screenshots, then compare store conversion and retention by locale.
8. Post short vertical gameplay clips showing a crowded late-game battle, a dramatic bomber attack, and a before/after upgrade comparison. Reuse them on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, VK Clips, Reddit, and relevant Discord communities.
9. Create an itch.io page and a simple indexed landing page with the exact Google Play link, screenshots, a 15–30 second trailer, and a press kit.
10. Track the funnel weekly: store impressions → listing visitors → installs → day-1 retention → uninstall rate. Fix the earliest weak step instead of changing everything at once.
